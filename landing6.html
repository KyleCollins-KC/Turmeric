Sample preparation for UV-Vis in ethanol

Scroll down to see how all the samples were prepared ready for UV-Vis analysis

Step 1

Approximately 5 mg of metanil yellow and curcumin was weighed using a Mettler Toledo AB104 analytical balance into green 2 mL Eppendorf Safe-Lock tubes.

Figure 1: Video demonstration of weighing procedure for sample preparation

Step 2

550 µL of ethanol was added to the Eppendorf tube containg curucmin Where as 2 mL of ethanol was added to the Eppendorf tube containg metanil yellow. Both additions used a 100-1000 µL Starlab pipette.

Figure 2: video demostration of how the solvent was added to the Eppendorf tubes

Step 3

The samples were mixed for 60 seconds at 2000 RPM using Fisher Scientific FB15012 TopMix This step was necessary because static interactions caused the turmeric to cling to the walls of the Eppendorf tubes, and mixing ensured full contact between the sample and the solvent

Figure 3: video demostration of the samples being mixed

Step 4

The samples were then ultrasonicated using a Fisher Scientific FB15046 ultrasonic bath for 15 minutes at room temperature. Which maximise the solubility of compounds that are soluble under these conditions

Figure 4: video demostration of the ultrasonic treamtment the samples received

Step 5

Following this, the samples were centrifuged for 10 minutes at 14,000 RPM and 20°C using a Beckman Allegra X-22R Benchtop Centrifuge

Figure 5: video demostration of the samples being centrifuged

Step 6

The supernatant was then carefully collected to avoid disturbing the solid at the bottom using a 20–200 µL Eppendorf Research pipette.

Figure 6: video demostration of the supernatant being collected

Step 7

The collected supernatant of both curcumin and metanil yellow was diluted by adding 10 µL to 990 µL of ethanol (using a 2-20 and 100-1000 µL starlab pipette), with curucmin being further diluted by adding 100 µL to 900 µL of ethanol (using a 20-200 µL Eppendorf Research pipette and a 100-1000 µL starlab pipette).

 

TU009 preperartion

The powder was washed off the surface of TU009 with approximately 600 µL of ethanol using a glass pasteur pipette. Then diluted by adding 100 µL to 900 µL of ethanol (using a 20-200 µL Eppendorf research pipette and a 100-1000 µL starlabs pipette), followed by 250 µL in 750 µL of ethanol (using a 100-1000 µL starlabs pipette).

 

Step 8

The UV-Vis data was collected using either the Hitachi U-3310 or Hitachi U-3900 spectrophotometer operating in absorbance mode, with a scan speed of 300 nm/min and a slit width of 2 nm. Spectra were recorded across different wavelength ranges, with the maximum range spanning 190–800 nm. Quartz cuvettes with a 1 cm path length were used for all measurements, and baseline correction was performed using ethanol blank.
