Sample preparation for the UV-Vis calibration curves

Scroll down to see how the standards were prepared for the UV-Vis calibration curves

Curcumin standard

Curcumin standard: A 2.07 mM curcumin stock was prepared by dissolving 3.81 mg (weighed using a Mettler Toledo AB104 analytical balance ) of curcumin in 5 mL of DMSO (≥99.9% purity from Sigma-Aldrich) using a 5 mL volumetric flask. 2 mL of DMSO was added to an 2 mL green Eppendorf Safe-Lock tube. followed by replacing an equal volume of DMSO with 26.25 µL of the curcumin solution (using a 2-20 µL starlab pipette ) forming a 27.1 µM solution. Twofold dilutions using DMSO were performed forming 13.6, 6.8, 3.4, 1.70 and 0.85 µM solutions. The final volume of all the solution was 1 mL except for 0.85 µM which was 2 mL.

 

Lead chromate standard

A 3.47 mM lead chromate stock was prepared by dissolving 0.0112 g of lead (II) chromate (weighed using Mettler Toledo AB104 analytical balance) in 10 mL of 1 M HCL (from 37% HCL)using a 10 mL volumetric flask requiring 3 minutes of ultrasonication (using a Fisher Scientific FB15046 ultrasonic bath ). 2 mL of the lead chromate stock solution was added to a 2 mL green Eppendorf Safe-Lock tube followed by replacing an equal volume of the lead chromate stock with 0.18 mL of 1 M HCl (using a 20-200 µL Eppendorf research pipette ) forming a 3.16 mM solution. Alternating half and fivefold dilutions were performed to obtain 1.58, 0.32, 0.16, 0.032, 0.016, and 0.0032 mM solutions. The final volumes of the solutions were all corrected to be 1 mL.
