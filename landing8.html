Sample preparation for SEM-EDX and ICP-MS analysis

SEM-EDX analysis sample preparation

Approximately 25 mg of all powders (weighed using a Mettler Toledo AB104 analytical balance ) , including 46% and 9.4% (w/w) TU002 spiked with lead chromate, were placed in a 48-well Nuclon surface plate and dried at 40°C for 2 days (using a Genlab oven ). About 2 mg of each sample was placed on aluminium SEM stubs lined with conductive carbon tape and then sputtered with gold (using a SC7640 SEM Sputter Coater ). The SEM-EDX data was recorded using a Gemini SEM 300 equipped with an Oxford Instruments Ultim Max detector. The working distance was maintained between 8.2–8.6 mm, with an electron high tension (EHT) of 20.00 kV and an aperture size of 60.00 μm.

 

ICP-MS sample preparation

Approximately 100 mg of samples TU001-TU0027 were digested using 60% ultrapure nitric acid (with a Ultrawave microwave digestion system , which can operate up to 199 bar and 300°C), with the final volume being made up to 10 mL. The ICP-MS data were recorded using an iCAP™ TQ ICP-MS./a>
